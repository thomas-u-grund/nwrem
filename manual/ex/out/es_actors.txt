. estat actors, role(sender) noplot

Observed and expected events by actor (an extension of nwrem; relevent has no
such method).
Expected = sum over the events of the model probability that the actor is the
sender (receiver); under the waiting-time likelihood, the summed rate integrals.

Pearson residual = (observed - expected) / sqrt(expected); the sums describe the
fit, they are not a test (events are not independent draws given the effects).
