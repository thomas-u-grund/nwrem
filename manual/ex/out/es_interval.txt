Observed versus expected over time (an extension of nwrem; relevent has no such
method).
Waiting-time likelihood: observed = number of events in the bin of consecutive
events; expected = sum of the integrated rates of the fitted model over the
intervals that end with those events (the last bin also carries the final
interval up to endtime()). The model has no baseline rate, so a level that is
far off says the effects cannot reproduce the overall event rate.
