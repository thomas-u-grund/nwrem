. estat time, bins(8) noplot

Observed versus expected over time (an extension of nwrem; relevent has no such
method).
Ordinal likelihood (it has no time scale): observed = number of events whose
observed dyad is among the 10 most likely dyads of the risk set (ties split);
expected = sum of the probabilities of the 10 most likely dyads.
